How to confirm

Four steps before the first visit

Call the number on your card

Ask: “Is a doula a covered benefit, and under which billing codes?”

Ask about the recommendation

Many plans need a recommendation from a licensed provider. Doula’s House can request it from your care team in one tap.

Check your doula is enrolled

Coverage usually requires a credentialed, in-network or enrolled doula.

Write it down

Note the date, the person and a reference number. It settles disputes later.
