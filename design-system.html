Design system · v2

Warm, clinical-grade calm.

The component library behind the identity: tokens, forms, data and privacy patterns for a care-coordination product. For strategy, the mark, voice and applications, see the full brand identity.

Principles

Held, not hurried

Generous space, slow reveals, soft shadows. Nothing shouts — except warnings, which are the only red on the page.

Editorial meets clinical

Fraunces carries brand emotion; Instrument Sans carries information. Never set data or forms in the serif.

Privacy is visible

Locks, consent toggles and audit rows are first-class components, not buried settings.

Colour

Core and brand colours come straight from the deck's photography and merch. Status colours are muted so they sit in the warm palette.

Core & brand

Status

Contrast: body text is Ink on Paper/Cream (≥ 15:1). Clay passes AA on Paper and Cream (4.7:1+). Gold, Honey and Dawn are graphic colours — use Umber or Dawn deep for text. Gold is decorative or ≥ 18px only — use Umber for gold-toned text. Camel text appears only on Mocha backgrounds.

Merch & commerce

Accessibility & motion

  • Focus3px gold ring with 3px offset on every interactive element.
  • TargetsMinimum 40px tall (52px for primary CTAs); 44px circular icon buttons.
  • MotionReveals ≤ 800ms, ease-out. All animation is removed under prefers-reduced-motion.
  • LanguagePlain, warm, second-person. No jargon where a person is the audience; precise terms where a clinician is.
  • ImageryReal, dignified portraits of Black and brown mothers; no stock-photo clichés; always with meaningful alt text.
