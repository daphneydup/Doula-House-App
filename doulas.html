Billing & privacy

Claims need some health information, so billing runs under strict rules: only the minimum data a payer requires goes on a claim, clients are shown by initials and member-ID suffix in your dashboard, and billing records are separate from your private visit notes.

Note: Billing codes, rates and rules differ by state and payer. The product maps them from a verified rules service; always confirm requirements with the plan.
