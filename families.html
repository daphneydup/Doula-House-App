Urgent warning signs — during pregnancy and up to a year after birth

Call 911 or your provider right away if you have any of these. Trust your instincts — and say “I'm pregnant” or “I recently had a baby.”

  • Severe headache that won't go away
  • Dizziness or fainting
  • Changes in vision
  • Trouble breathing or chest pain
  • Fast-beating heart
  • Severe belly pain
  • Heavy vaginal bleeding or discharge
  • Swelling, redness or pain in a leg
  • Fever of 100.4°F or higher
  • Thoughts of harming yourself or your baby

Source: CDC “Hear Her” urgent maternal warning signs. This list is not exhaustive and is not a substitute for medical care.
