Privacy & trust

Your story is yours.

A birth plan can include health information, so privacy isn't a footer link here — it shapes how every screen works. These are the principles the product is built on.

Principles

Twelve guardrails

1 · Consent before access

Nothing is visible to anyone until the mother grants it — per person, per section, with an end date.

2 · Minimum necessary

Each role sees only what it needs. A doula sees the plan she's been given, not the medical chart.

3 · Role-based access control

Clinical and non-clinical roles are enforced by the system, not by good manners. Clinical sections can't be shared with non-clinical accounts.

4 · Audit logging

Every view, share, revoke, export and sign-in is recorded. Patients can see their own access log.

5 · Break-glass emergency access

In an emergency a clinician can request time-boxed access with a stated reason. It's logged, limited to clinical sections, reviewed afterwards and the patient is notified.

6 · No PHI in notifications

Emails, texts and push alerts say “you have a new message” — never what it says or who it concerns.

7 · Encryption everywhere

Encrypted in transit and at rest, with strong authentication and multi-factor sign-in for professionals.

8 · Auto-lock & auto-expire

Idle sessions lock after a short period. Shared access ends automatically on the date the patient sets.

9 · Business Associate Agreements

When we handle protected health information for a practice, we sign a BAA, and so do our vendors that touch that data.

10 · Separate worlds

The merch store and scholarship applications never touch health data, and the care app never shares data with marketing.

11 · Billing: data on a diet

Claims include only what a payer requires. Doula visit notes stay private to the doula unless she chooses otherwise.

12 · Your data, your call

Export your plan, revoke everyone, or delete your account. Retention follows legal requirements and is explained plainly.

Honest limits

No software can make a practice compliant on its own. HIPAA also depends on people and policies — training, access reviews, incident response. We aim to make the safe way the easy way, and we'll be clear about what we do and what remains with each practice.

Prototype notice: this site is a design prototype with fictional data. Before launch, the privacy policy, terms and these principles must be reviewed by qualified healthcare-privacy counsel and backed by a HIPAA-eligible infrastructure and security assessment.
